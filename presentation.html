Project Overview & Defense

Antara: AI-Powered Multilingual Mental Health Journaling Platform

Bridging Linguistic Disparities in Digital Wellbeing with Indic Transformer Architectures & Calm Computing Design.

Multilingual & Code-Mixed

Native script and Romanized transliterations across English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Malayalam (Hinglish, Tanglish, Manglish, Tenglish).

Shared Multitask NLP

Jointly predicts sentiment polarity, 6-class emotional categorization, and continuous valence regression using fine-tuned Google MuRIL.

Ethical & Non-Diagnostic

Strict user data sovereignty, deterministic Tele-MANAS crisis routing, and a 36-hour forgiving habit matrix.

Problem Statement

The Linguistic Barrier in Digital Mental Health

Standard digital mental health tools assume formal English, alienating millions in multilingual societies.

The Code-Switching Dilemma: In conversational digital environments, users express vulnerable emotions using colloquial code-mixing:
"Today college la romba stressful ah irundhuchu, but friends kooda pesina apram konjam better feel panninen."

Why Existing Solutions Fail

Monolingual BERT and standard English LLMs misinterpret phonetic transliterations, mistaking colloquial slang for noise or negative sentiment.

Stigma & Alarmist Design

Traditional apps gamify mental health with punitive streak resets and diagnostic over-reach, generating user anxiety rather than psychological relief.

System Architecture

Three-Tier Calm Computing Architecture

Stateless, low-latency, and architected for user privacy and rapid inference.

Core NLP Innovation

Shared-Encoder Multitask MuRIL Network

Unified representation learning across three complementary affective tasks.

Task 1: Sentiment

Cross-entropy loss over Positive, Neutral, Negative classes with slang normalization.

Task 2: Emotion

Categorical cross-entropy over 6 granular states: Joy, Calm, Hopeful, Sadness, Stress, Anxiety.

Task 3: Valence

Continuous Mean Squared Error (MSE) regression bound between -1.0 and +1.0.

Joint Multi-Task Objective: L_total = α * L_sentiment + β * L_emotion + γ * L_valence
Sharing the MuRIL encoder reduces inference memory by ~60% while boosting emotion Macro-F1 by +7.9%.
Platform Capabilities

Humane, Non-Alarmist User Features

Engineered for longitudinal emotional clarity and nervous system regulation.

Longitudinal Trajectory Analysis

Tracks 7-day, 30-day, and 3-month emotional valence rolling averages. Linear regression slope analysis detects continuous downward shifts to offer gentle wellness prompts.

60s Guided Breathing Space

Immediate self-regulation modal powered by Web Audio API harmonic Solfeggio soundscapes synthesized entirely client-side without external network streams.

Forgiving Habit Matrix

Replaces punitive streaks with a 36-hour grace day window, reinforcing positive journaling habits without triggering guilt or anxiety.

Calendar Reflection Archive

Full-text search, language filters, and multi-entry color-coded emotional badges mapped directly to calendar days.

Responsible AI

Ethical Boundaries & Privacy Safeguards

Proactive safeguards designed to protect vulnerable users.

Non-Diagnostic Mandate

Antara offers emotional reflections, not clinical diagnoses or psychiatric labels.

Deterministic Crisis Triage

Multilingual regex patterns detect severe distress and route immediately to Tele-MANAS (14416) and KIRAN.

Data Sovereignty

1-click JSON/CSV journal export, password hashing with bcrypt, and GDPR-compliant cascading account deletion.
